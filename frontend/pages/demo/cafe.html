Frequently asked questions

Yes. Drinks, brunch, cakes, lunch menus and specials can all be presented clearly.

Can customers send table enquiries?

Yes. The enquiry form is designed around bookings, events and general cafe enquiries.

Is this suitable for independent cafes?

Yes. The layout works for local cafes, coffee shops, bakeries and brunch spots.

Can online ordering be added later?

Yes. Online ordering, booking systems and payment integrations can be added later.
