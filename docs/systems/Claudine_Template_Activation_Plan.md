# Claudine Template Activation Plan

## Purpose

Claudine supports many industries, but templates must be activated in controlled phases.

The goal is to avoid building too many unfinished templates at once.

## Phase 1 — Master Templates

Build three high-quality master templates first:

### 1. Service Business Master
For:
- plumber
- electrician
- builder
- cleaning
- roofing
- repair
- local services

Goal:
Turn visitors into calls, quote requests, bookings, and enquiries.

### 2. Premium Clinic / Wellness Master
For:
- clinic
- dentist
- therapist
- salon
- spa
- wellness
- physiotherapist
- chiropractor

Goal:
Turn visitors into consultations, bookings, and premium client enquiries.

### 3. Creator / Personal Brand Master
For:
- creator
- influencer
- artist
- musician
- podcaster
- streamer
- educator
- author
- coach

Goal:
Turn attention into followers, subscribers, bookings, launches, events, and product sales.

## Phase 2 — Industry Variants

After the master templates are stable, create variants:

- law firm
- accountant
- real estate
- restaurant
- hotel
- photographer
- videographer
- marketing agency
- recruitment
- pet services

## Phase 3 — Premium Ecosystem Templates

Later create premium templates for:

- luxury clinics
- high-end creators
- private services
- exclusive events
- boutique brands
- ecosystem-linked experiences

## Phase 4 — Automation & Analytics

Attach:

- lead tracking
- conversion analytics
- CRM workflows
- follow-up automation
- DataAxis insights
- accessibility scoring

## Rule

Do not activate more than three templates at once.

Complete, test, and commit each template before starting another.
