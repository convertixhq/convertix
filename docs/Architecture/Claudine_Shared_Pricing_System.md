# Claudine Shared Pricing System

## Purpose

Pricing sections help visitors understand value clearly without confusion or pressure.

## Pricing Types

### Service Pricing
Used for:
- trades
- salons
- restaurants
- local businesses

Examples:
- quote-based pricing
- package pricing
- service tiers

### Consultation Pricing
Used for:
- clinics
- therapists
- wellness
- premium services

Examples:
- free consultation
- priority consultation
- booking deposit
- premium session

### Creator Pricing
Used for:
- creators
- musicians
- educators
- influencers
- podcasters

Examples:
- memberships
- digital products
- launches
- coaching sessions
- premium access

## Pricing Rules

Pricing must feel:
- transparent
- premium
- understandable
- non-manipulative

Avoid:
- fake discounts
- misleading urgency
- fake countdowns
- confusing tiers

## Mobile Rules

Pricing cards should:
- stack cleanly
- remain readable
- support large touch targets
- preserve CTA visibility

## Accessibility

Pricing layouts must support:
- readable typography
- strong contrast
- keyboard navigation
- screen-reader structure

## Rule

Pricing should create clarity and confidence, not pressure.
