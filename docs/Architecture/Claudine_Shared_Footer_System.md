# Claudine Shared Footer System

## Purpose

The footer gives visitors final trust, clarity, navigation, and legal reassurance.

## Core Footer Elements

Reusable footer blocks may include:

- brand logo
- short brand statement
- contact links
- service links
- legal links
- privacy policy
- terms
- accessibility statement
- social links
- ecosystem links

## Footer Types

### Service Business Footer
For local businesses, clinics, trades, restaurants, salons.

### Creator Footer
For creators, musicians, podcasters, authors, educators.

### Premium Footer
For luxury clinics, boutique brands, private services.

## Footer Rules

The footer should be:

- clean
- readable
- calm
- legally useful
- not overcrowded

## Accessibility

Footer links must be easy to tap on mobile and readable with strong contrast.

## Rule

The footer should quietly reinforce trust without distracting from the main conversion journey.
