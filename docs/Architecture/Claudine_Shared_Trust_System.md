# Claudine Shared Trust System

## Purpose

Claudine trust systems help visitors feel safe enough to take action.

## Trust Elements

Reusable trust blocks may include:

- reviews
- testimonials
- certifications
- accreditations
- response-time promises
- secure enquiry messaging
- privacy notes
- payment transparency
- business credentials
- before/after examples where legally appropriate

## Industry Examples

### Clinics / Wellness
- consultation clarity
- professional standards
- privacy reassurance
- treatment suitability disclaimer

### Trades / Local Services
- insurance
- qualifications
- service areas
- emergency availability
- customer reviews

### Creators
- audience proof
- press mentions
- previous launches
- social proof
- booking clarity

### Law / Professional Services
- confidentiality
- experience
- regulated status
- clear enquiry boundaries

## Rule

Trust must be real, clear, and verifiable. Never fake proof.
