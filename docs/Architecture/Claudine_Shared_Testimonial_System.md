# Claudine Shared Testimonial System

## Purpose

Testimonials provide social proof and help visitors feel confident enough to take action.

## Testimonial Types

### Service Business Testimonials
Used for:
- plumbers
- electricians
- builders
- salons
- restaurants
- local services

Focus:
- reliability
- professionalism
- response time
- quality of work

### Clinic & Wellness Testimonials
Used for:
- clinics
- dentists
- therapists
- wellness providers

Focus:
- trust
- comfort
- professionalism
- experience quality

### Creator Testimonials
Used for:
- creators
- musicians
- educators
- coaches
- influencers

Focus:
- audience trust
- community value
- transformation
- experience quality

## Display Formats

Reusable layouts:
- quote cards
- sliding testimonials
- video testimonials
- before/after context blocks
- trust grids

## Accessibility

Testimonials must:
- remain readable
- avoid autoplay overload
- support keyboard navigation
- preserve contrast and spacing

## Rule

Testimonials must be authentic, relevant, and believable.

Never use fake reviews or fabricated proof.
