# Claudine Project Doctrine

Claudine is the luxury digital growth and conversion infrastructure layer within the wider ecosystem.

## Purpose

Claudine helps premium clinics, aesthetic brands, wellness providers, and service businesses convert attention into booked consultations, paid appointments, and structured client journeys.

## Positioning

Claudine is not a generic marketing agency.

Claudine is a premium client-experience system combining:

- high-converting landing pages
- consultation funnels
- booking flows
- payment-ready journeys
- trust layers
- analytics
- automation
- future AI-assisted optimisation

## Ecosystem Role

Claudine acts as the luxury business gateway brand.

It may later connect traffic and intelligence across:

- ViralWave
- Masheyla
- Summera
- DataAxis

## Folder Purpose

- brand/ — logos, colours, voice, identity rules
- marketing/ — landing copy, outreach scripts, campaign material
- systems/ — funnel logic, conversion flows, booking architecture
- analytics/ — tracking plans, metrics, DataAxis integration notes
- automation/ — future lead automation, CRM flows, AI assistants
- frontend/ — website interface
- backend/ — future API, database, auth, lead storage
- demo/ — live prototype funnel pages
- docs/ — permanent project documentation
- infra/ — deployment and hosting structure
