# Claudine Legal Doctrine

## Purpose

Claudine must operate as a transparent premium digital infrastructure platform.

Trust, clarity, and legal discipline are core requirements.

## Principles

- no misleading claims
- no fake urgency
- no hidden payment handling
- no deceptive medical promises
- transparent consultation flows
- clear booking expectations

## Data Handling

Claudine must later support:

- GDPR compliance
- consent-based data collection
- secure lead storage
- deletion requests
- privacy-first handling

## Payment Structure

Current prototype:
- Formspree demo flows only

Future systems:
- Stripe
- clinic-direct payment processing
- transparent booking deposits
- no hidden transaction routing

## Clinic Responsibility

Clinics remain responsible for:
- medical advice
- treatment suitability
- appointment handling
- patient outcomes
- legal compliance in their country

Claudine provides:
- digital infrastructure
- booking flows
- conversion systems
- automation layers

## Future Legal Documents

Later additions may include:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Cookie Policy
- Lead Processing Agreements
- Clinic Partnership Agreements
- Refund & Booking Policies

## Ecosystem Considerations

If connected later to:
- ViralWave
- Summera
- Masheyla
- DataAxis

each platform must maintain separate legal boundaries and policies.

## Rule

Premium trust is built through clarity and discipline, not aggressive tactics.
